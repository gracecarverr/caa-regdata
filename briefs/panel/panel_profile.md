## 1. Scale & coverage

Both panels are, by construction, a **balanced facility × year rectangle** over 2015–2025 (11 years) — every
facility present has a row in every year, since continuity (`ACTIVE_BROAD == 1` in all 11 years, `PB2`) is
the entry condition, not an outcome measured after the fact.

| panel | facilities | facility-years | balanced |
|---|--:|--:|---|
| `major_synmin_continuous_2015_2025` | 20,261 | 222,871 | TRUE |
| `electric_continuous_2015_2025` | 1,913 | 21,043 | TRUE |

`panel_facility_count_over_time.png` visualizes this directly — both series are flat lines across 2015–2025,
which doubles as a build-correctness check, not just a display of scale (a non-flat line would mean the
continuity screen didn't actually hold for that build).

## 2. `OBS_SOURCE` composition — event vs. operating

`OBS_SOURCE` can only be `"event"` (an ICIS record exists that year) or `"operating"` (no ICIS event, but
confirmed active another way) in these panels — `"unobserved"` never appears, by construction (`PB4`; checked
by the script every run, not just asserted here).

The **share of facility-years with an actual ICIS event** (`pct_event`) declines over the window for both
panels, most sharply in the final year:

| panel | 2015 | 2018 (peak) | 2021 | 2025 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| major_synmin | 81.0% | 90.5% | 76.7% | **72.5%** |
| electric | 91.3% | 96.4% | 87.8% | **81.4%** |

Electric utilities carry a materially higher event-coverage rate than the broader major/synmin population in
every single year (a ~10–15 point gap) — consistent with electric generating units facing denser ICIS
touchpoints (more frequent inspections/reporting) than the major/synmin population as a whole. Both panels dip
together in 2025, the most recent year, which is at least partly a right-censoring/reporting-lag artifact (the
most recent year is the least likely to have every event fully entered into ICIS yet) rather than necessarily
a real drop in regulatory activity — worth keeping in mind before reading 2025 as a genuine trend point.

## 3. Key count measures (`ICIS_OBSERVED` subset)

Computed on the `ICIS_OBSERVED == 1` subset within each panel (175,944 major_synmin facility-years; 18,697
electric facility-years) — the same zero-vs-NA gate as the dataset layer (`13_regulatory_profile.R`).

| measure | major_synmin median / mean / pct zero | electric median / mean / pct zero |
|---|--:|--:|
| Inspections | 1 / 1.85 / 24.7% | 1 / 2.49 / 27.5% |
| Violations | 0 / 0.17 / 88.8% | 0 / 0.13 / 91.6% |
| — of which HPV | 0 / 0.05 / 97.6% | 0 / 0.04 / 97.8% |
| Enforcement (pooled) | 0 / 0.42 / 85.9% | 0 / 0.26 / 90.5% |
| — Formal | 0 / 0.11 / 93.8% | 0 / 0.08 / 95.5% |
| — Informal | 0 / 0.31 / 89.2% | 0 / 0.19 / 93.1% |
| Certifications | 5 / 5.00 / 36.2% | 5 / 7.39 / 14.8% |
| Stack tests | 0 / 1.39 / 75.5% | 2 / 3.65 / 59.8% |
| Penalty actions | 0 / 0.10 / 94.6% | 0 / 0.07 / 96.0% |

Electric utilities inspect more often (higher mean, lower pct-zero on inspections) but see *less* enforcement
and fewer violations/HPVs per observed facility-year than the broader major/synmin population — more scrutiny,
proportionally fewer findings. Certifications and stack tests are both markedly higher for electric (mean 7.39
vs. 5.00 certs; mean 3.65 vs. 1.39 stack tests, and electric's stack-test median is 2 vs. major_synmin's 0) —
expected, since continuous emissions monitoring and periodic stack testing are a standard Title V/NSPS
obligation for fossil generating units specifically. `panel_count_distributions.png` shows the full ECDFs
side by side (pseudo-log x-axis) — electric's inspection/enforcement curves sit visibly below major_synmin's
at low counts and cross over in the tail, consistent with "more facilities get at least one inspection, but
the heaviest-enforcement tail is thinner."

## 4. HPV-active rate over time

Both panels show a **steady decline in HPV-active share**, and both decline together, not independently:

| panel | 2015 | 2020 | 2025 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| major_synmin | 4.54% | 3.78% | **2.31%** |
| electric | 5.02% | 4.34% | **1.99%** |

Roughly a halving over the 11-year window for both — matches the direction (though not necessarily the exact
magnitude or mechanism) already documented for the dataset layer in `hpv_profile.md`'s Figure 1. Electric runs
slightly *above* major_synmin most years through ~2022 before crossing below it by 2025 — a reversal in the
final two years that's a small enough gap (74 vs. 630, 38 vs. 468 active facility-years respectively) to be
worth treating as noise rather than a real crossover until confirmed on a later refresh.

## 5. `PENALTY_AMOUNT`

`PENALTY_AMOUNT` is `NA` if no formal action occurred that facility-year *or* if it summed to exactly $0 —
both read the same (documented, unresolved ambiguity carried over unchanged from `data/panels/README.md`).
Non-`NA` values only:

| panel | n non-NA | pct NA | median | p95 | max | total |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| major_synmin | 9,437 | 95.8% | $9,800 | $298,196 | $43.1M | $1.16B |
| electric | 751 | 96.4% | $8,000 | $159,500 | $4.9M | $58.3M |

Electric's median penalty ($8,000) is close to major_synmin's ($9,800), but its distribution is much shorter
in the tail — max $4.9M vs. $43.1M — meaning the largest settlements in this space are concentrated outside
electric generating units specifically. **These totals are naive** — no dup-row or multi-facility-settlement
broadcast correction is applied here (same caveat as `12_penalties_profile.R`'s `by_year.csv`); don't cite
the $1.16B/$58.3M totals as a corrected figure. `panel_penalty_dist.png` shows both distributions on a shared
log10 axis, faceted by panel (separate y-axis scales given the ~14x size difference in n).

## 6. Facility characteristics (facility-level)

**Pollutant class** — electric is far more Major-Emissions-heavy than the broader panel, as expected given
NAICS 2211/SIC 4911 utilities tend to be large combustion sources:

| panel | Major | Synthetic Minor |
|---|--:|--:|
| major_synmin | 53.3% | 46.7% |
| electric | 85.4% | 14.6% |

**Geography (top states, facility count)** — largely overlapping, with California a notably bigger share of
electric than of major_synmin:

| major_synmin top 5 | | electric top 5 | |
|---|--:|---|--:|
| TX | 6.05% | CA | 8.36% |
| PA | 5.63% | TX | 7.79% |
| IN | 4.80% | NY | 5.96% |
| CA | 4.63% | KS | 4.76% |
| NC | 4.12% | IL | 4.23% |

**EPA Region** — major_synmin is led by Region 4 (22.7%) and Region 5 (18.7%); electric is more evenly spread
across Regions 5 (17.7%), 4 (15.2%), 7 (14.1%), and 6 (12.6%), with no single region dominating the way Region
4 does for major_synmin. `panel_state_composition.png` plots the top 10 states (major_synmin's ranking, both
panels' bars) for a direct visual comparison — see the figure's own caveat (§ FLAGGED ISSUES in the script)
that electric's bars use major_synmin's state ordering, not its own top 10.

**Ownership** — both panels are dominated by privately-owned facilities (76.7% major_synmin, 71.7% electric);
electric shows a materially larger municipal share (10.9% vs. 2.1% for major_synmin) — consistent with the
prevalence of municipal power utilities.

## 7. Coordinate coverage & geocoding quality

| panel | pct has coordinate | pct gross error (of evaluable) | dist p95 (km) | dist max (km) |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| major_synmin | 97.5% | 1.3% | 0 | 1,617.8 |
| electric | 98.7% | 0.8% | 0 | 336.9 |

Both panels sit well above the full-universe dataset-4 coordinate coverage rate (84.5%, `coordinates_profile.md`)
— expected, since both panels are restricted to continuously-active, currently-classified facilities, a
population skewed toward better-maintained FRS records than the full 279,665-facility universe. Electric's
lower gross-error rate and much smaller max distance (336.9km vs. 1,617.8km) likely reflect a smaller, more
homogeneous, better-curated facility population rather than anything panel-construction-specific.

## 8. Entry/exit censoring

Within the 2015–2025 window every facility here is active in all 11 years by construction — these columns
describe activity **outside** the window, not gaps within it:

| panel | pct left-censored | pct right-censored | pct confirmed-exited | entered-year median |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| major_synmin | 94.2% | 99.5% | 0.5% | 2015 |
| electric | 91.9% | 99.6% | 0.4% | 2015 |

The overwhelming majority of facilities in both panels are **left-censored** (existed before the observation
window with an unknown/pre-window begin year) and **right-censored** (no confirmed exit, still active as of
the last observed year) — expected for a continuity-screened panel of long-lived industrial facilities, and
consistent with the entered-year median landing right at the window's start (2015) for both panels: most
facilities' true entry predates what this panel can observe.

## 9. The subset relationship

`code/04_panel_building/README.md` documents electric as major_synmin's filter **plus** NAICS 2211/SIC 4911 —
confirmed directly: **every one of the 1,913 electric facilities is also a major_synmin facility** (checked
every script run, not just asserted). Electric is 9.4% of major_synmin's facility count.

## Notable things to sanity-check before relying on these panels for a headline number

- **`OBS_SOURCE` composition drifts down in 2025 for both panels** (§2) — plausibly a reporting-lag artifact
  in the most recent year rather than a real activity drop; don't treat 2025 as a clean trend endpoint without
  checking this on a later refresh once 2025 has had time to fully populate in ICIS.
- **`PENALTY_AMOUNT`'s NA-vs-exactly-$0 ambiguity** (§5) is inherited, unresolved, and affects both the
  `pct_na` figures here and any downstream analysis that treats `PENALTY_AMOUNT` as "amount, or genuinely
  none" — it can't currently distinguish "no formal action" from "formal action, zero dollar penalty."
- **Every dollar total here is naive** (§5) — no dup-row or multi-facility-settlement broadcast correction,
  same caveat as the dataset-layer `penalties_profile.md`/`multi_facility_settlement_decision.md`.
- **The 2024–2025 HPV-active crossover between panels** (§4) is based on small facility-year counts (74 vs.
  38 active facility-years) and shouldn't be read as a real reversal without confirming it persists on a later
  data refresh.
- **Electric's state/region composition is genuinely different from major_synmin's**, not just scaled down
  (§6) — California and municipal ownership are both disproportionately represented in electric relative to
  the broader panel; don't assume electric is a uniform random subsample of major_synmin along any dimension
  other than the NAICS/SIC filter itself.
