Facility × Year Panels

Panels

Two continuously screened facility × year panels for empirical work on enforcement and compliance, both spanning 2015–2025: a broad Major / Synthetic Minor panel and a narrower Electric utilities subset. Construction decisions, coverage, and summary statistics below.

1. Scale & coverage

Both panels are, by construction, a balanced facility × year rectangle over 2015–2025 (11 years) — every facility present has a row in every year, since continuity (ACTIVE_BROAD == 1 in all 11 years, PB2) is the entry condition, not an outcome measured after the fact.

panel facilities facility-years balanced
Major/Synthetic Minor 20,261 222,871 TRUE
Electric 1,913 21,043 TRUE

Facility counts are flat lines across 2015–2025 for both panels, which doubles as a build-correctness check, not just a display of scale (a non-flat line would mean the continuity screen didn't actually hold for that build).

2. OBS_SOURCE composition — event vs. operating

OBS_SOURCE can only be "event" (an ICIS record exists that year) or "operating" (no ICIS event, but confirmed active another way) in these panels — "unobserved" never appears, by construction (PB4; checked by the script every run, not just asserted here).

The share of facility-years with an actual ICIS event (pct_event) declines over the window for both panels, most sharply in the final year:

panel 2015 2018 (peak) 2021 2025
major_synmin 81.0% 90.5% 76.7% 72.5%
electric 91.3% 96.4% 87.8% 81.4%

Electric utilities carry a materially higher event-coverage rate than the broader major/synmin population in every single year (a ~10–15 point gap) — consistent with electric generating units facing denser ICIS touchpoints (more frequent inspections/reporting) than the major/synmin population as a whole. Both panels dip together in 2025, the most recent year, which is at least partly a right-censoring/reporting-lag artifact (the most recent year is the least likely to have every event fully entered into ICIS yet) rather than necessarily a real drop in regulatory activity — worth keeping in mind before reading 2025 as a genuine trend point.

4. HPV-active rate over time

Both panels show a steady decline in HPV-active share, and both decline together, not independently:

panel 2015 2020 2025
major_synmin 4.54% 3.78% 2.31%
electric 5.02% 4.34% 1.99%

Roughly a halving over the 11-year window for both — matches the direction (though not necessarily the exact magnitude or mechanism) already documented at the dataset layer. Electric runs slightly above major_synmin most years through ~2022 before crossing below it by 2025 — a reversal in the final two years that's a small enough gap (74 vs. 630, 38 vs. 468 active facility-years respectively) to be worth treating as noise rather than a real crossover until confirmed on a later refresh.

5. PENALTY_AMOUNT

PENALTY_AMOUNT is NA if no formal action occurred that facility-year or if it summed to exactly $0 — both read the same (documented, unresolved ambiguity carried over unchanged from the underlying panel data). Non-NA values only:

major_synmin 9,437 95.8% $9,800 $298,196 $43.1M $1.16B
electric 751 96.4% $8,000 $159,500 $4.9M $58.3M

Electric's median penalty ($8,000) is close to major_synmin's ($9,800), but its distribution is much shorter in the tail — max $4.9M vs. $43.1M — meaning the largest settlements in this space are concentrated outside electric generating units specifically. These totals are naive — no dup-row or multi-facility-settlement broadcast correction is applied here (same caveat noted at the dataset layer); don't cite the $1.16B/$58.3M totals as a corrected figure. Both distributions sit on a shared log10 axis when faceted by panel (separate y-axis scales given the ~14x size difference in n).

8. Entry/exit censoring

Within the 2015–2025 window every facility here is active in all 11 years by construction — these columns describe activity outside the window, not gaps within it:

panel pct left-censored pct right-censored pct confirmed-exited entered-year median
major_synmin 94.2% 99.5% 0.5% 2015
electric 91.9% 99.6% 0.4% 2015

The overwhelming majority of facilities in both panels are left-censored (existed before the observation window with an unknown/pre-window begin year) and right-censored (no confirmed exit, still active as of the last observed year) — expected for a continuity-screened panel of long-lived industrial facilities, and consistent with the entered-year median landing right at the window's start (2015) for both panels: most facilities' true entry predates what this panel can observe.

9. The subset relationship

Electric is defined as major_synmin's filter plus NAICS 2211/SIC 4911 — confirmed directly: every one of the 1,913 electric facilities is also a major_synmin facility (checked every script run, not just asserted). Electric is 9.4% of major_synmin's facility count.

Notable things to sanity-check before relying on these panels for a headline number

  • OBS_SOURCE composition drifts down in 2025 for both panels (§2) — plausibly a reporting-lag artifact in the most recent year rather than a real activity drop; don't treat 2025 as a clean trend endpoint without checking this on a later refresh once 2025 has had time to fully populate in ICIS.
  • PENALTY_AMOUNT's NA-vs-exactly-$0 ambiguity (§5) is inherited, unresolved, and affects both the pct_na figures here and any downstream analysis that treats PENALTY_AMOUNT as "amount, or genuinely none" — it can't currently distinguish "no formal action" from "formal action, zero dollar penalty."
  • Every dollar total here is naive (§5) — no dup-row or multi-facility-settlement broadcast correction, same caveat noted at the dataset layer.
  • The 2024–2025 HPV-active crossover between panels (§4) is based on small facility-year counts (74 vs. 38 active facility-years) and shouldn't be read as a real reversal without confirming it persists on a later data refresh.
  • Electric's state/region composition is genuinely different from major_synmin's, not just scaled down (§6) — California and municipal ownership are both disproportionately represented in electric relative to the broader panel; don't assume electric is a uniform random subsample of major_synmin along any dimension other than the NAICS/SIC filter itself.
