Facility × Year Panels

Panels

Two facility × year panels for empirical work on enforcement and compliance, both spanning 2015–2025 and screened to facilities active in at least one year of the window: a broad Major / Synthetic Minor panel and a narrower Electric utilities subset. Construction decisions, coverage, and summary statistics below.

1. Scale & coverage

Both panels are still a full facility × year rectangle over 2015–2025 (11 years) — build_panel()'s expand_grid gives every eligible facility one row per year regardless of which year(s) made it eligible (code/04_panel_building/03_build_functions.R). What changed under PB2 is the eligibility rule itself: a facility now qualifies with just one qualifying year (ACTIVE_BROAD == 1 for at least one of 2015–2025), not all eleven — so the facility population is far larger than the old continuity-screened panels.

panel facilities facility-years balanced
Major/Synthetic Minor 45,873 504,603 TRUE
Electric 2,965 32,615 TRUE

Compare to the retired continuity-screened build: 20,261 / 1,913 facilities respectively (36.3% of the 55,777-facility CONUS+major/synmin candidate set passed the old all-11-years rule; 82.2% pass the new ≥1-year rule). Facility counts are flat lines across 2015–2025 for both panels, that invariant survives the rule change because eligibility is evaluated once over a facility's whole record, not per year, so the set of facilities in the panel can't vary by year regardless of which rule is used.

2. OBS_SOURCE composition — event vs. operating vs. unobserved

OBS_SOURCE can now genuinely be "event", "operating", or "unobserved" — under the old all-11-years rule "unobserved" was impossible by construction (every row had already passed ACTIVE_BROAD==1 for that year); under the ≥1-year rule a facility can qualify via one year while other years in its 11-year rectangle have no confirmed activity (PB4, panel_construction_decisions.md). That share is now substantial — 12–49% of facility-years depending on year and panel (output/panel_profile/coverage_by_year.csv).

panel 2015 2018 2021 2025
major_synmin — event 47.3% 45.8% 45.5% 44.7%
major_synmin — unobserved 23.6% 48.9% 14.0% 12.1%
electric — event 71.0% 69.3% 65.1% 60.5%
electric — unobserved 10.0% 26.8% 10.2% 13.0%

Both series are much flatter than the retired build's event-share table (which ran 72–96%, since that population was pre-selected for full-window activity). 2018 stands out as an anomaly in both panels — pct_unobserved roughly doubles or triples relative to the surrounding years (and pct_operating correspondingly collapses to near-zero, coverage_by_year.csv), which was invisible under the old continuity screen precisely because it filtered out every facility that could expose it. Confirmed cause: 2018 has no real Wayback snapshot — the raw folder was a mislabeled duplicate of 2019 and was removed from data/raw/ (2026-07-21, see code/02_cleaning/wayback/README.md); operating/prog_*_active are asserted explicit NA for every facility that year (not LOCF-filled, since there's no real observation for any facility to infer from). That NA propagates into ACTIVE_BROAD's wayback component (O6, dataset_construction_decisions.md), which is why the "operating" (wayback-confirmed-but-no-ICIS-event) share collapses and "unobserved" absorbs it — a known, structural data gap for this one year, not a panel-construction defect.

8. Entry/exit censoring

This section changes the most substantively of any in this brief, and is the direct, intended consequence of the PB2 revision. Under the old all-11-years screen, every facility was active through 2025 by construction, so right-censoring sat near 100% and confirmed exits near 0% — the panel could not see a facility leave mid-window. Under the ≥1-year rule, facilities that exit the sample partway through are now included, and that's exactly the survivorship-bias fix this change was meant to produce:

panel pct left-censored pct right-censored pct confirmed-exited entered-year median
major_synmin 72.2% 87.4% 10.6% 2015
electric 82.3% 86.7% 10.5% 2015

Compare to the retired build: 94.2%/99.5%/0.5% (major_synmin), 91.9%/99.6%/0.4% (electric). Confirmed exits rose roughly twentyfold — this is the panel now actually containing the population of facilities that enforcement/penalty analysis needs to see, rather than conditioning them away. Left-censoring also dropped (72.2%/82.3% vs. 94.2%/91.9%), consistent with the broader population skewing toward facilities with more completely-observed lifecycles within the window, not just long-lived survivors.

9. The subset relationship

Electric is defined as major_synmin's filter plus NAICS 2211/SIC 4911 — confirmed directly: every one of the 2,965 electric facilities is also a major_synmin facility (checked every script run, not just asserted). Electric is 6.5% of major_synmin's facility count (down from 9.4% under the retired build — the ≥1-year rule added proportionally more non-electric major/synmin facilities than electric ones).

Notable things to sanity-check before relying on these panels for a headline number

  • 2018 is a known, structural gap, not comparable to other years (§2, §4). pct_unobserved roughly doubles or triples in 2018 relative to the surrounding years in both panels, and the same pattern shows up in HPV-active coverage — caused by 2018 having no real Wayback snapshot at all (mislabeled duplicate of 2019, removed from data/raw/; code/02_cleaning/wayback/README.md). This was invisible under the old continuity screen, which filtered out every facility that would have exposed it. Don't read a 2018 dip in any ACTIVE_BROAD-derived measure as a real activity change.
  • OBS_SOURCE/HPV-active-rate levels are not comparable to the pre-2026-07-29 versions of this brief — the denominator population changed substantially (55,777-candidate-set pass rate 36.3% → 82.2%), so a lower observed rate here reflects a broader, less-selected population, not a real decline relative to the old numbers.
  • PENALTY_AMOUNT's NA-vs-exactly-$0 ambiguity (§5) is inherited, unresolved, and affects both the pct_na figures here and any downstream analysis that treats PENALTY_AMOUNT as "amount, or genuinely none" — it can't currently distinguish "no formal action" from "formal action, zero dollar penalty."
  • Every dollar total here is naive (§5) — no dup-row or multi-facility-settlement broadcast correction, same caveat noted at the dataset layer.
  • Electric's state/region composition is genuinely different from major_synmin's, not just scaled down (§6) — don't assume electric is a uniform random subsample of major_synmin along any dimension other than the NAICS/SIC filter itself.
